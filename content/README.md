## Rules

- Preserve original German wording whenever it is available.
- Record the original source URL for every page.
- Mark missing information clearly as missing; do not guess or leave ambiguous gaps.
- Do not invent business information, including products, prices, opening hours, policies, history, contact details, legal text, or product descriptions.
- Every source of original content must be reviewed and approved by the business owner before publication.
- Use explicit placeholders for all unverified company details, such as `[UNVERIFIZIERTER FIRMENNAME]`, `[UNVERIFIZIERTE ANSCHRIFT]`, `[UNVERIFIZIERTE ÖFFNUNGSZEIT]`, `[UNVERIFIZIERTER PREIS]`, `[UNVERIFIZIERTE LIEFERBEDINGUNG]` and `[UNVERIFIZIERTER RECHTLICHER HINWEIS]`.

- Do not claim that any placeholder or demonstration information is verified or approved.
- No content is publication-ready until the business owner has reviewed and approved it.

## Use

Place collected and approved content in the corresponding Markdown template. Draft information must not be treated as approved until the `Owner approval` field is completed and the content is reviewed.
